So I keep building sites with AI tools and they always look done. Then I deploy and something's broken - SSL cert expiring, mobile layout overflowing, missing meta tags. The usual suspects.

Got tired of manually checking each thing, so I built Livecheck.

It's a CLI tool. You point it at any deployed site:

npx livechecks https://yoursite.com

And it runs a checklist - DNS, SSL, headers, SEO, mobile responsiveness, page speed - then gives you a score out of 100. You know exactly what's broken before your users find it.

The stack is TypeScript with Playwright for real browser rendering and Cheerio for HTML parsing. One thing I was deliberate about: the AI analysis feature only sends check results to the provider, never your page content or credentials. Privacy first.

Also the exit code is 0 when clean, 1 when something fails. So it just works in CI - no custom wrapper needed.

Live at livechecks.netlify.app. Run it against your next deploy and see what comes back.
