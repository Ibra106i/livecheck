# Brag Plan: Livecheck

## What is this app?
Livecheck is a productized rescue layer for marketing agencies — it runs a fixed technical checklist (SSL, DNS, SEO, forms, speed, mobile) against any AI-built website, scores it, auto-patches ~80% of issues, and delivers a white-label SLA certificate. Built for the last mile of AI-built sites.

## The angle
AI website builders ship fast — then break at launch. Livecheck catches what broke and fixes most of it before a human ever looks. The joke: AI gets you 80% there, and the other 20% is where agencies burn billable hours on one-off firefighting. Livecheck turns that chaos into a productized pipeline.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The stats: "1,240+ AI-built sites rescued" slam in, with the grid background and emerald glow. Fast, bold, numbers-first. The viewer sees scale before they see the product.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The intake form — URL field, client name, "Run Website Scan" button with arrow. This is the product entry point, not a landing page.
- The scan animation — pulsing emerald icon, "Scanning website..." with the URL. Real waiting moment.
- The results card — score bar fills, PASS/WARN badges cascade in one by one. SSL, DNS, SEO, Mobile checks land with visual weight.

## Outro / punchline
"80% auto-patched before a human ever looks." Then the product name and tagline: "Livecheck — Last-Mile Rescue for AI-Built Websites." Clean landing, no joke needed — the stat IS the punchline.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry** — Type URL into intake form, click "Run Website Scan"
2. **Scan** — Pulsing scan animation with real site URL shown
3. **Result** — Score bar fills, check categories cascade in with PASS/WARN/FAIL badges

## Tone
- Preset: default
- Creative direction: playful but credible agency tool — not a joke product, but not taking itself too seriously
- Interpretation: Comfortable pacing with room for each moment to breathe. Clean transitions, readable type, and the product's own dark emerald palette. Humor comes from the problem (AI breaks things at launch) not from trying to be funny.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #030712 (deep navy-black)
- Accent: #06d6a0 (emerald green) — primary action color
- Secondary accent: #60a5fa (blue) — used for secondary elements
- Text: #f1f5f9 (light slate) on dark
- Muted text: #94a3b8
- Display font: Inter (800 weight for headlines)
- Body font: Inter (400-500 weight)
- Strongest visual element: The audit results card with score bar and cascading PASS/WARN/FAIL status badges — this is the product's signature moment

## Share copy (draft)
AI ships the site. We close the last mile — 1,240+ sites rescued, 80% auto-patched before human review.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, modern electronic bed — clean and professional but not sterile
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (upbeat, most energetic, good for default tone)
- Music treatment: start at 0.3s fade-in, volume at 0.35, gentle fade-out at end
- Music cue guidance: to be detected at composition time via hyperframes beats
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use music RMS/bass to make the emerald glow and score bar presence breathe. No waveform/equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: moderate — UI clicks for form interactions, scan pulse for the scanning moment, success chime for results
- Audio-coupled moments: form field focus sound, scan pulse matching animation, cascading check badges with light drop sounds, score bar fill with rising tension
- Restraint rule: no aggressive SFX, no glitch sounds — this is a credible tool, not a meme

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook: The Problem in Numbers — 3s
Dark background with subtle grid. The stat "1,240+" slams in large Inter 800, followed by "AI-built sites rescued" in smaller text below. Emerald glow pulses gently around the number. The grid is barely visible — just enough to give depth.
Sequential/interaction: stat number appears first (0.3s), label fades in after (0.5s delay)
Audio intent: one soft impact hit on the number arrival, then the music bed kicks in
Audio-coupled idea: number slams with impact/impactSoft_medium_000, music starts at 0.3s
Music: upbeat, clean, starts low and builds
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The Concept: "80% There" — 4s
Hero text from the site: "Your AI website builder gets you" (small, muted) then "80% there." (large, emerald gradient) then "We close the last mile." (medium, white). Type arrives in three beats, each with a slight upward motion. Background: deep navy with radial emerald/blue gradient glow.
Sequential/interaction: three text lines arrive one by one, each 0.8s apart
Audio intent: each text line gets a soft interface/drop sound on arrival
Audio-coupled idea: three sequential interface/drop_001.ogg at 0s, 0.8s, 1.6s relative to scene start
Music: continues, steady
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The Flow: Intake Form — 4s
Recreated intake form: dark card with "Site URL" field (showing "clientsite.com"), "Client Business Name" field ("Acme Consulting"), and a green "Run Website Scan →" button. The button gets a simulated click — it depresses and the arrow shifts right. This is the product doing its thing, not describing it.
Sequential/interaction: form fields appear one by one (0.4s each), then button click at 2.5s
Audio intent: soft focus sound on field appearance, click sound on button press
Audio-coupled idea: interface/click_001 on button press, fields use interface/drop_002
Music: continues, energy holds
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The Scan: Working — 3s
The scan screen: pulsing emerald scan icon (ScanSearch from lucide), "Scanning website..." text, "clientsite.com" shown below. The icon pulses twice. Real waiting moment — this is the product working.
Sequential/interaction: scan icon appears, pulses at 0.5s and 1.5s, text fades in
Audio intent: subtle digital pulse sound synced to icon pulse
Audio-coupled idea: interface/glitch_002 at reduced volume for scan pulses (two hits)
Music: continues, slight tension build
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — The Payoff: Results — 4s
The results card: score bar fills from left to right (0→81 in emerald). Below: four check categories appear one by one — SSL (PASS, green), DNS (PASS, green), SEO (WARN, amber), Mobile (PASS, green). Each has a small icon and status badge. This is the payoff — the product delivered.
Sequential/interaction: score bar fills over 1.5s, then four check badges cascade in at 0.4s intervals
Audio intent: score bar fill has a rising tension, each badge arrival gets a light success sound
Audio-coupled idea: score fill uses a subtle rising tone, badges use interface/drop_001 at each arrival
Music: builds to a gentle peak
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Outro: Name + Tagline — 2s
"Livecheck" in large Inter 800, emerald gradient. Below: "Last-Mile Rescue for AI-Built Websites" in muted text. Clean, confident, no clutter. The product name is the final beat.
Sequential/interaction: product name appears first, tagline fades in 0.3s later
Audio intent: one clean accent on the name, music fades out
Audio-coupled idea: impactBell_heavy_000 on name reveal (soft, not aggressive)
Music: fades out over 1s
Transition mood: fade to black
